Our Foundation

Beth Muturi's
Vision for Mukuru

Founded on August 30, 2017, The Rising Mukuru began with a single act of courage — creating a safe space for vulnerable children and young women in one of Nairobi's most underserved communities.

"She saw girls facing gender-based violence. She saw youth without mentorship. She saw potential being crushed by poverty — and she decided to act."

— On Beth Muturi, Founder

Today, we run mentorship programs, mental health sessions, empowerment workshops, and community events — reaching young lives every single day in Mukuru kwa Reuben.

Real Transformation.

Meet Beth's Vision

Founded on August 30, 2017, The Rising Mukuru began as a simple act of hope: creating a safe space for vulnerable children and young women in our community.

Beth Muturi saw the gaps. She saw girls facing gender-based violence. She saw youth without mentorship. She saw potential being crushed by poverty and hopelessness.

She decided to take action. Today, we run mentorship programs, mental health sessions, and community workshops—reaching young lives every single day.

Our dream? A stronger, safer Mukuru where every child and woman has hope, confidence, and real opportunity.

What We Do

🤝

Mentorship Programs

Connecting vulnerable youth with role models who believe in them. Real guidance. Real relationships. Real transformation.

💚

Mental Health Support

Safe spaces to process trauma, build resilience, and reclaim mental wellbeing. Because healing matters.

🍽️

Basic Needs Access

Nutritious meals, shelter support, and essential resources—meeting immediate needs so young people can dream bigger.

👩‍🎓

Empowerment Workshops

Skills training, financial literacy, and gender-based violence prevention—building strong foundations for independent futures.

🎯

Community Engagement

Activating the neighborhood. Street football tournaments, art sessions, dance events—joy is part of the healing.

🌟

Leadership Development

Identifying emerging leaders among youth and young women—building the change-makers our community needs.

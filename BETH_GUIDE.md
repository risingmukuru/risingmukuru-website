# Beth's Guide — How to Update the Rising Mukuru Website

*This guide is written for you, Beth. No technical knowledge needed. If you can use WhatsApp, you can do this.*

---

## What You Can Edit

You have your own editing dashboard at:

**`https://risingmukuru.org/admin`**

From that one page, you can update:

- Your contact details and social media links
- The homepage hero text (the big words at the top)
- The impact numbers (500+ Youth Reached, etc.)
- The About section — your story
- Program descriptions
- Community stories and testimonials
- Donation amounts and what they fund
- Volunteer page content

---

## Step 1 — Log In

1. Go to `https://risingmukuru.org/admin` in your browser
2. Click **"Login with GitHub"**
3. Sign in with the Rising Mukuru GitHub account (Maestro will set this up for you and share the password)
4. You'll land on a clean, simple dashboard — it looks like this:

```
⚙️ Site Settings
🏠 Homepage
📋 Programs
💬 Impact Stories
💳 Donate Page
🙋 Volunteer Page
```

That's it. Click whatever you want to edit.

---

## Step 2 — Make a Change

Let's say you want to update the phone number:

1. Click **⚙️ Site Settings**
2. Click **📞 Contact & Organisation Info**
3. Find the **Phone Number** field
4. Type in your new number
5. Click **Publish** (the blue button at the top right)

Done. The website updates automatically within about 2 minutes.

---

## Adding a New Community Story

1. Click **💬 Impact Stories**
2. Click **"New Impact Story"** (top right)
3. Fill in:
   - **Person's Name** — just first name + initial is fine (e.g. "Amina W.")
   - **Description** — e.g. "Age 17 · Youth Mentorship Programme"
   - **Their Story** — write it in first person, as if they are speaking. Keep it real. Keep it short (3–5 sentences is perfect).
   - **Photo** — optional. If you have a photo, click "Choose Image" and upload it.
4. Make sure **Active** is switched ON (green toggle)
5. Click **Publish**

The story will appear in the carousel on the homepage.

---

## Adding a New Program

1. Click **📋 Programs**
2. Click **"New Programs"**
3. Fill in:
   - **Program Title** — the name (e.g. "Sports & Recreation")
   - **Icon** — paste an emoji (e.g. ⚽)
   - **Number** — the order (07, 08, etc.)
   - **Description** — 2–3 sentences about what the program does
   - **Active** — switch ON to show it, OFF to hide it
4. Click **Publish**

---

## Updating Your Impact Numbers

1. Click **🏠 Homepage**
2. Click **📊 Impact Numbers**
3. Change the numbers to reflect your latest data
4. Click **Publish**

*These are the big numbers on the homepage — Youth Reached, Lives Empowered, Girls Protected, Years of Impact.*

---

## When You Get Social Media Pages

When Facebook, Instagram, or WhatsApp pages are created:

1. Click **⚙️ Site Settings → Contact & Organisation Info**
2. Scroll down to the social media URL fields
3. Paste in the full link (e.g. `https://facebook.com/RisingMukuru`)
4. Click **Publish**

The icons on the website will automatically become clickable.

---

## When You Have a Contact Email and Phone

Same process — **⚙️ Site Settings → Contact & Organisation Info** — fill in the Email and Phone fields and click Publish. The footer of every page will update automatically.

---

## Important Rules

- **Always click Publish** after making changes — if you close the tab without publishing, changes are lost.
- **Don't edit the file names** of existing entries (programs, stories) — this can break links.
- **Photos:** best size for community stories is a square photo. Best size for the About section is a tall (portrait) photo.
- If something looks wrong after you publish, contact Maestro — he can check the GitHub history and undo any mistake.
